Before the Roar — a synthetic football moment, explained and corrected.

Watch a fictional match moment move through BTR's five-stage workflow. A viewer challenges the crew, Truth Lab changes a synthetic defender record, and the revised explanation preserves the passing facts that still hold. Current Understand, Publish and Accessibility Director views are included.

The cinematic minute and measured synthetic replay are separate sources. The film is not calibrated tracking footage. This edited demo uses recorded public-app sessions; voice and captions were added in post. The correction tests evidence dependence, not football causation. It does not claim general broadcast-video recognition or independently proven commentary superiority.
