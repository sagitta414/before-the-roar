BEFORE THE ROAR — 105-SECOND PRODUCT DEMO
Open /?tour=1&demo=1#watch and select Play the 105-second tour.
This is an actual application walkthrough. Saved model contributions and reviewed explanations are labeled; do not call them a fresh AI run.

0–18 seconds — WATCH
Football can be hard to read before the shot. Before the Roar makes the same play understandable to a newcomer, an analyst and a numbers-focused fan. This replay uses fictional players and synthetic coordinates.

18–36 seconds — DISCUSS
The crew has different jobs. Scout spots the movement. Guide makes it understandable. Vector calculates. Check challenges the claim. Host connects the pieces. The live room now streams each contribution, lets Guide respond to Scout, and supports follow-up rounds with saved context.

36–52 seconds — MEASURE
These completed passes gain twenty-three point seven metres toward goal. Open the calculation: every number points back to its event. This is progress through passing, not a chance-of-scoring claim.

52–72 seconds — CHALLENGE
Now change the defender's tracking record. The movement claim is withdrawn. The completed passes remain. The caption and explanation follow the corrected evidence. Trust comes from being able to change the story when the source changes.

72–95 seconds — PERSONALIZE
A beginner gets an explanation of what to watch. A numbers-focused viewer gets the calculation and its limits. The same facts are available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

95–105 seconds — CLOSE
Azure hosts the intelligence and saves the editorial work. The public source, measured evaluation and viewer study are available. Before the Roar: one play, different perspectives, a story you can question.
