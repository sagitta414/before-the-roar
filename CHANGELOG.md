## v42 / API 3.8.0 — football value and clearer evidence

- Concrete football briefs compare the wide run with the central passing route, a recovery with the forward outlet, and the build-up with the shot outcome. The mathematical contribution uses passing gain, pressure distance or time from recovery to shot, with units and source IDs.
- Scout observes, Guide supplies a definition and viewing question, Vector calculates, Check qualifies the claim, and Host closes with an implication and replay cue. Fresh discussions use four model requests instead of six; supplied context is distinguished from a model-selected tool call.
- Corrections survive all five audience modes in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Beginner and math captions retain the withdrawal while leaving valid pass measurements intact.
- Model HTTP 429 responses trigger a bounded cooldown before any further reservation. Raw accepted and rejected outputs are retained. The guard rejects explicit claims of no influence when the evidence merely fails to prove causation, including the actual adverb phrasing caught in live recovery.
- Match Centre puts its pitch ahead of secondary statistics. Truth Lab puts its claim and challenge ahead of chapter controls. Conversation spacing is tighter without reducing body text size. Production Desk opens the current recorded evidence.
- A voluntary three-question self-check records actual answers and task time locally and supports JSON export. No human study, engagement improvement or time-saving result is claimed.
- Live recovery reused Scout and Guide: three requests before interruption, one afterwards. It also exposed an unsupported Host sentence; that receipt is retained with an explicit failed editorial review. The final guard repair invalidates earlier cached discussions.
- Earlier incomplete evaluations are preserved. Comparative guard acceptance, raw outputs and limitations remain visible; a small engineering comparison is not evidence of semantic superiority.
- Final v42.2 comparison: four fixed cases across three scenarios and three languages; team 11/12 accepted outputs with 16 requests and 22.1 seconds total execution, baseline 12/12 with four requests and 12.5 seconds. One corrected Spanish Host response fell back. The pacing intervals are excluded from execution totals. No quality advantage over the baseline is established.
- Generated footage remains an edit of separate shots. Automatic video eventing, real-world calibration, human outcome testing and final participant submission remain outstanding.
